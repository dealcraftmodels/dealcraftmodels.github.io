What we do

DealCraftModels creates digital financial modeling tools and Excel templates for entrepreneurs, small businesses, investors, and acquisition professionals. Our products help you analyze investments, plan financing, and make better business decisions — directly in Microsoft Excel.

Financial calculators

Practical Excel calculators for everyday business finance: loan amortization, ROI / IRR / NPV investment analysis, break-even analysis, and business valuation. Also available as a discounted bundle.

Acquisition & investment models

Professional-grade Excel models for search funds and acquisition professionals: ETA acquisition models, DCF valuation, letter-of-intent evaluation, deal tracking, investment committee memos, and due-diligence checklists.
